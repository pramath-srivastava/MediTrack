A STRAIGHTFORWARD WORKFLOW

How MediTrack works

A straightforward workflow for organizing medication schedules and reviewing your activity.

01

Create your account

Choose Patient or Caregiver access and create an account with your name, email, and password.

You provide: your account details and role. MediTrack stores: account and profile information for sign-in and settings.

02

Add medicines

For a patient account, enter the medicine name, dosage, dates, and any useful instructions.

You provide: medicine and schedule details. MediTrack displays: your active medication list and upcoming doses.

03

Build your schedule

Choose daily, weekday, weekly, or selected-day schedules and add one or more times.

You choose: schedule dates, days, and times. MediTrack uses: those settings to show the planned doses.

04

Record doses

Mark a scheduled dose as taken or skipped when you update your routine.

You record: dose activity. MediTrack displays: the dose status in your day view and history.

05

Review history

Use the history view to review recorded and missed dose activity over time.

You can review: dates, medicines, times, and statuses. Historical activity remains visible according to current app behavior.

06

Understand adherence

Review the application’s adherence analytics to see recent patterns.

MediTrack displays: a summary of dose activity for the available reporting period.

07

Connect a caregiver

A patient can invite a caregiver who has their own caregiver account. The caregiver signs in and accepts using the invitation flow. Either side can revoke access through the app.

Access is limited: caregiver visibility follows the existing server authorization and connection controls.

IMPORTANT

What MediTrack does not do

MediTrack does not diagnose, prescribe, recommend dosages, or make medical decisions. It is an organizing and tracking tool.
