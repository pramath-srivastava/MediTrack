Privacy Policy

Informational draft: Project owner and legal professional should review before production use.

Information handled

Account creation collects name, email, password, role, and timezone. Profiles include name and timezone. Patient accounts may store medicines, dosage, schedules, instructions, and dose history. Caregiver invitations and connections are stored.

Sessions and security

Authentication uses server-managed HTTP-only cookies, CSRF protections, origin validation, and role-based API authorization. Application data is stored in MongoDB. Hosting and database services follow project deployment configuration.

Notifications

Browser notifications require user permission and work while the page remains open.

Data requests and updates

Use the project established support channel for questions. No retention period or self-service account deletion process is documented here. This policy may be updated.
