Privacy Policy

A summary of information handled by the current MediTrack application.

Last updated: 2026

This page is general website and app information. The project owner and a legal professional should review it before production use.

1. Overview

This policy describes information handled by the MediTrack application as currently implemented. Hosting and service details may vary according to the project’s deployment configuration.

2. Information you provide

Account creation collects your name, email address, password, selected account role, and timezone. Passwords are processed by the existing server authentication implementation and are not returned as profile information. Profile settings include name and timezone.

3. Medication and schedule information

Patient accounts can store medicine names, dosage details, instructions, start and end dates, schedule frequency, selected weekdays, and scheduled times.

4. Dose and history information

The app records dose dates, times, and statuses such as taken or skipped, and displays history and adherence summaries based on those records.

5. Caregiver information

The application handles caregiver account details, invitations, connection status, and access relationships. Patient and caregiver access is controlled by the existing server authorization.

6. Authentication and cookies

Sign-in uses the existing server-managed session in an HTTP-only cookie. CSRF protections and origin validation are part of the existing server behavior. The browser cannot read the HTTP-only session cookie.

7. How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, medication scheduling, dose recording, history, analytics, profile settings, and caregiver access features.

8. Data storage

Application data is stored in MongoDB through the configured application deployment. The specific provider and location depend on project environment settings.

9. Security measures

The implementation includes server-side authentication and authorization, HTTP-only cookies, CSRF protection, origin validation, and password hashing. These measures do not guarantee absolute security.

10. Browser notifications

Browser notifications require permission from your browser. The current application notes that notifications work while its page is open.

11. Third-party and deployment services

The project uses configured hosting and MongoDB services. The public pages load the DM Sans font and Chart.js from external web services. Refer to the deployment configuration and those providers’ notices for their data practices.

12. Retention and deletion

This repository does not document a general retention period or a self-service account deletion feature. Contact the project through its established support channel for information requests.

13. Changes to this policy

This page may be updated as MediTrack changes. The updated text will be published here.

14. Contact

Use the project’s established support/contact channel. No support email is configured in this repository. See Contact & Support.
