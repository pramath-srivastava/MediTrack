Medical Notice

MediTrack is an organizing and tracking tool.

  • Replace a doctor, pharmacist, or qualified clinician

Your medication information

You are responsible for entering accurate medicine and schedule information. Check the details you enter and follow the instructions provided by your healthcare professional.

Medical questions and urgent situations

Speak with a qualified healthcare professional about your medicines, dosage, or treatment. For urgent or emergency medical situations, contact appropriate emergency or medical services.
